How We Started

Built By Norwich Mechanics, For Norwich Drivers

GoGo Tyre started in 2012 with a single ramp in a Mile Cross lock-up and a simple idea: fair prices, honest advice, and no unnecessary upselling. Word travelled fast around Norwich, and within three years we'd outgrown that first workshop.

Today we run a fully-equipped diagnostic workshop and a fleet of mobile fitting vans covering the whole of Norfolk — but we've kept the same independent, no-nonsense approach that got us started. Every technician we hire is local, and every price we quote is the price you pay.
